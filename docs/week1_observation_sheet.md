## Key Findings

Our group evaluated Claude AI, a conversational AI assistant developed by Anthropic, focusing on safety, instruction-following, and system limitations.

Our first finding is that Claude demonstrates effective safety guardrails. When we requested instructions for unauthorized account access, Claude refused to provide harmful information and suggested legitimate alternatives such as account recovery and IT support.

Our second finding is that Claude demonstrates transparency about its knowledge limitations. When asked about recent events, Claude explained its knowledge cutoff and acknowledged that information obtained through web searches may be incomplete or unreliable.

Our third finding concerns instruction-following. When asked "Is Python good?", Claude assumed that Python referred to the programming language without clarifying the intended meaning. Although the response was informative, it demonstrated a potential weakness in ambiguity handling.

The most serious Responsible AI risk we identified is safety guardrail failure. Although Claude successfully rejected our harmful test prompt, more complex or disguised requests could potentially challenge its safety mechanisms.

Overall, Claude performed well in our tests, particularly in safety and transparency. However, improvements in ambiguity detection and continuous safety evaluation could further enhance its reliability.
